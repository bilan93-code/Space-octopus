Space Octopus actuator PCB | 23 November 2026 revision
Request for DFM and pricing | Five prototype assemblies

Quote a single-board design with two-sided SMT and through-hole assembly.
Please return the proposed stackup, panelization, DFM findings and component
matching / placement preview for customer review before tooling or assembly.

Fabrication
  Four layers, 1.60 mm finished thickness and 2 oz outer copper. Confirm inner
  copper weights. The board has a 112 mm outer diameter, a routed 28 mm center
  opening and six nonplated 4.50 mm mounting holes. The project uses 0.15 mm
  minimum tracks and 0.20 mm clearance. The smallest via has a 0.20 mm drill
  and 0.40 mm land. Confirm drill and registration tolerances for the stackup.
  Include bare-board electrical test. Provide filled, copper-capped and
  planarized via-in-pad wherever a via intersects a solderable SMT land,
  including the array at F2 pin 1. Keep component and harness holes open.

Assembly
  The top face points away from the motor. Review the stencil on both faces,
  component polarity, pin 1, bottom-side rotation and through-hole lead trim.
  F2 is the proposed Littelfuse 0463015.ER, 15 A, LCSC C720194. Quote it as a
  directly soldered SMD component. The proposed pad and paste geometry and
  final part selection require customer approval. No cartridge or holder is
  fitted. There is no actuator BOM position for the upstream F1 fuse or the
  external 4.7 ohm +/-5%, 300 W resistor.

Customer-installed wire harness
  Leave all seven 1.80 mm nominal finished plated wire holes solderable.
  J1   1 = DC+ / VBUS, from an externally fused feed     2 = supply GND
  J2   1 = U     2 = V     3 = W
  J11  1 = BR+ / brake fuse output    2 = BR_SW / switched brake return
  The separate braking-resistor PCB connects across the two J11 wires.

Part matching
  Confirm live stock, assembly allowance and all exact manufacturer order
  codes. Obtain customer approval for substitutions. Check the C5 film
  capacitor case and seating fit and verify D2 and D3-D5 pin assignments.
