 Motor design and
 Gmsh–Elmer analysis
 Space Octopus • 12-slot / 10-pole permanent-magnet motor • 6 November 2026




 This report extends the previous Pyleecan/FEMM dimension report with actual three-dimensional geometry,
 electromagnetic finite elements, insulated-wire thermal calculations, complete motor conduction, and rotor
 structural calculations. Gmsh prepares the geometry and meshes; Elmer performs the field solves. CalculiX
 supplies the orthotropic rotor and contact analyses.

 The deliverable is a simulation design candidate for independent validation. Supplier magnetic curves,
 composite strength, adhesive temperature/creep properties, bearing data and manufacturing tolerances
 remain material design inputs. Numerical completion does not constitute a fabrication or hardware
 qualification.

 Torque finding: at 12.2 Arms and 120°C magnets, the eight-position 3D mean is 4.9001 N·m. After the
 stated 18 W non-copper loss allowance, the estimated mean shaft torque is 4.6273 N·m against the 4.3651
 N·m requirement.

 Thermal limit: continuous 22 A hot holding reaches 154.7°C and fails the declared 130°C limit. The refined
 18.5 A case reaches 126.4°C. A five-second 22 A pulse from the 12.2 A hot holding field reaches 103.3°C
 under the stated cooling and material assumptions.

   Mesh                             Nodes                          Tetrahedra         Near-gap target [mm]

   fine                             141627                         803193             0.700

 The axial-half domain starts at the 42.5 mm midplane. Magnetic normal flux is zero on that symmetry
 plane; total torque and phase flux integrals are multiplied by two. The far cylinder radius is 90 mm and its
 outer axial plane is z = 155 mm. The complete closed coil end region is present. The nonmagnetic housing,
 liner, bond and wrap are replaced by air in static magnetics; exterior ferromagnetic fastener effects are a
 separate unresolved material scenario.

 The STEP rotor is at the nominal CAD reference, with pole 1 centred at 18°. The first magnetic case adds
 50.999972° mechanical rotation to that rotor while keeping stator/coils fixed. Its phase currents are [0,
 +√(3/2) Iq, −√(3/2) Iq]. Other sweep cases use electrical angle 5(θ−50.999972°). Preserve this reference
 when recreating the cases in Ansys; CAD zero and the first solved rotor position are different.

 The linear check uses relative permeabilities 1500 in the lamination plane and 19.75 axially. The nonlinear
 model uses the bundled M270-35A B–H curve and 95% steel fill, mixing parallel permeability and series
 axial reluctivity. The supplied curve ends at 1.8 T; higher-field extrapolation remains an unqualified
 material assumption.

 A boundary-rooted spanning-tree gauge removes every active gradient mode and is checked by graph
 rank. The native interior forest left one mode in this mesh. Failed/nonphysical configurations are excluded.
 Direct and iterative comparisons, mesh refinement, finite field checks and actual solver residuals are
 recorded; an Elmer process merely exiting successfully is insufficient.

 For linear tetrahedral Nedelec elements, A inside a cell is reconstructed as A at the centroid plus ½
 B×(r−rcentroid). Coil flux is then integrated with 64-point Duffy quadrature along the actual curved current
 direction. The 27-versus-64-point change is recorded to separate end-turn integration error from magnetic
 mesh error.

 With the accepted component method, coarse-to-fine linear torque changes 0.672% (4.411918→4.382280
 N·m); nonlinear torque changes 0.635% (4.472416→4.444000 N·m). The fine nonlinear ψd,rms is
 0.02721123 Wb. The independent discrete Maxwell torque differs from the component result by less than
 0.02% in the principal comparison cases. Local sharp-corner |B| maxima are less converged than integral
 torque, especially near the axial magnet seams.

 The approximately 3 T global maxima in the segmented cases occur in tiny air regions near the inner
 bond/seam edges, not as a uniform steel induction. At the 12.2 A/120°C reference position, the maximum
 homogenized steel |B| is about 1.686 T and its volume-weighted p99 is 1.303 T. Intrinsic lamination BFe can
 exceed bulk B because of fill. The near-zero d-axis fault torque is not a useful relative-error benchmark; its
 extraction difference is about 0.0016 N·m in absolute terms.

 result and adds a temperature sensitivity check.

 Constant 12.2 A meets the mean torque criterion under the stated assumptions, but the sampled trough
 after the 18 W drag allowance falls below the shaft requirement. A separate nonlinear 13 A solve at the
 lowest sampled position tests available headroom. A controller can use an angle/current lookup and
 feedback to compensate ripple; the table reports a linearized feasibility profile, not an implemented
 controller.

 The profile estimates Iq(θ) by scaling the sampled torque curve, then includes the resulting q-flux
 derivative in voltage. Cogging is not strictly proportional to current, and current-dependent cross-saturation
 is only approximated. Exact continuum extrema, sensor/commutation errors, dynamic current tracking and
 inverter limits require controller calibration. The constant 13 A thermal cases conservatively bound the
 heating of a profile that remains below 13 A.
